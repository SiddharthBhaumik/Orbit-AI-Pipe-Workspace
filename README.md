# Orbit

Orbit is a browser-based workspace for chatting with AI models through AI Pipe.

**Use Orbit:** https://siddharthbhaumik.github.io/Orbit-AI-Pipe-Workspace/

## How to use

1. Open Orbit and go to **Preferences**.
2. Visit https://aipipe.org/login and sign in to create an API token.
3. Paste the token into Orbit's **AI Pipe token** field and connect.
4. Choose an available model, enter your prompt, and send it. Use **Compare** to compare responses from two models.

Orbit offers three visual themes: Astral Indigo, Gothic Observatory, and Infernal Eclipse.

**Note:** Your token and chat history are saved in your browser. Keep your token private, especially on shared devices. Available models depend on your AI Pipe account and provider access.
